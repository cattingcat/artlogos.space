# Demonstration artwork sources

These images are temporary portfolio demonstration assets by **Sasha Mihajlovic**, sourced from the artist’s [Figures collection](https://www.sashamihajlovic.com/category/figures). They are copyrighted third-party artworks, not public-domain assets. No reuse license or permission is established by this source record. Replace them with the portfolio owner’s work or obtain the appropriate permission for a public launch.

The artist’s logo, brand assets, and fonts are not included. Prices are reference prices in GBP; availability is the artist’s visible shop status captured on 2026-10-04, not an offer from this demonstration portfolio. Sold works have a null price in the data.

Materials were checked against the individual product descriptions on 2026-10-04. Blue Moon, The Colours of Sicilia, and Amelia are oil pastels. Nunzio Over Lasagne uses oil paint over oil pastels. The remaining works are oil paintings.

Files retain the downloaded image/avif bytes and use the matching `.avif` extension, even though the CDN filenames end in `.jpg`. Each alternate is the corresponding second product image.
